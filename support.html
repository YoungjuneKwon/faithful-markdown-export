Support

One person builds and supports this app. That means you get an answer from someone who knows the code, and it means the answer is not instant.

Getting help

Email yjkwon@winm2m.com. We reply within two working days, Korea Standard Time (UTC+9).

Reporting an export that lost something

This is the report we most want to receive, and the one we can act on fastest.

  • What the page looked like in Confluence — a screenshot of the part that went wrong is enough.
  • What the exported Markdown said — paste the few lines, not the whole file.
  • Whether the degradation report mentioned it. If the report was silent, that is itself a bug: the app is supposed to say what it could not represent.

Please do not send us your actual documents. A description or a small reduced example is always enough, and we would rather not hold your content — the app never does.

Reproducing it without sharing anything

If you can make the problem happen on a page you build yourself, that page becomes a test case we keep forever. The conformance suite takes contributions: add the markup that breaks, open a pull request, and the rule stays in the suite for every release after that.

Reporting a security problem

Email yjkwon@winm2m.com privately rather than opening a public issue. We acknowledge within three working days.

Feature requests

Welcome, with one standing caveat: this app exports and does not import, and that is a deliberate limit rather than a missing feature. Doing one direction properly is what makes the quality claim possible for one person to keep.
